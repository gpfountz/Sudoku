Important OpenCV binary provenance qualification
The reviewed opencv-python-headless 5.1.0.95 macOS wheels contain 16
(ARM64) and 19 (Intel) native libraries. Their libavcodec reports
"LGPL version 2.1 or later" and was built from Homebrew ffmpeg-lgpl 9.0.2
without --enable-gpl. The wheels also bundle OpenSSL 4.1.0, X11/XCB, xz,
libvpx and dav1d, and on Intel also libaom, libavif and VMAF. The upstream
bundled notice has no sections for libX11, libXdmcp, xz or VMAF. Preserve it,
but do not treat it as a complete, verified license inventory for every native
library. The earlier 5.0.0.93 ARM64 wheel bundled a GPL version 3 or later
FFmpeg build; that discrepancy is tracked at:
https://github.com/opencv/opencv-python/issues/1260

Before publishing an app binary, resolve the exact licenses and matching
source/build materials for its bundled native libraries. Including these
notices alone does not complete GPL/LGPL source-distribution obligations.
See docs/licensing.md and docs/opencv-binary-audit.md in the source tree.
