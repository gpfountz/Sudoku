Third-party software in Sudoku for macOS

These upstream license texts are reproduced verbatim. They do not become
Greg Pfountz's copyrighted work or lose their original license terms.
The inventory records the reviewed versions, notice origins and SHA-256
hashes, and the native libraries present in the reviewed macOS wheels.

Python: Python Software Foundation license and historical notices.
PyObjC (core, Cocoa, Quartz, Vision): MIT license.
NumPy 2.5.4: BSD and additional licenses reproduced in the wheel notices.
OpenCV Python wrapper: MIT; OpenCV and embedded components: see the full
upstream LICENSE-3RD-PARTY text, including Apache, BSD, GPL and LGPL notices.
Setuptools 84.2.1 and its vendored helpers: MIT and additional bundled notices.
py2app launcher: MIT or PSF terms; the upstream text is included here.
Apple frameworks are supplied by macOS and are not redistributed here.

Important OpenCV binary provenance qualification
The reviewed opencv-python-headless 5.0.0.93 macOS ARM64 wheel contains
99 native libraries. Its libavcodec reports "GPL version 3 or later" and
was built with --enable-gpl and --enable-version3. The upstream bundled
notice describes FFmpeg as LGPL. Preserve that notice, but do not treat
it as a complete, verified license inventory for every native library.
This discrepancy is tracked at:
https://github.com/opencv/opencv-python/issues/1260

Before publishing an app binary, resolve the exact licenses and matching
source/build materials for its bundled native libraries. Including these
notices alone does not complete GPL/LGPL source-distribution obligations.
See docs/licensing.md and docs/opencv-binary-audit.md in the source tree.
